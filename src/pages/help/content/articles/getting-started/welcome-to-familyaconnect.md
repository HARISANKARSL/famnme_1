FamNme helps you build, explore, and preserve your family tree with tools designed for Indian families — but useful for anyone. This guide gets you to your first working tree in about five minutes.

## What you can do here

- Build an interactive family tree on a visual canvas, with unions (marriages) and generations laid out automatically.
- Capture **memories, stories, and daily moments** against specific relatives.
- Invite family members to **collaborate**, with fine-grained roles (Viewer, Controller, Co-owner).
- Explore **heritage tools**: migration maps, timelines, temples, relationship paths, and more.
- Keep everything in one private space — only people you invite can see your tree.

## The 5-minute first run

1. **Create your account** — email + password, Google sign-in, or a passkey. See [Creating your account](/help/getting-started/creating-your-account).
2. **Start your tree** — on the dashboard, open the *Create tree* wizard and add yourself as the *home person*. Generations are calculated relative to this node.
3. **Add your immediate family** — parents, spouse, children. Open the *+ Add* menu on any person card.
4. **Upload a photo or two** — drag into the person modal, or paste from clipboard.
5. **Invite someone** — a parent or sibling is a great first invite. They can fill in the parts of the tree they know better than you.
