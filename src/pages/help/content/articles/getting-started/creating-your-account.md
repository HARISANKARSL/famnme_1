FamNme supports three ways to create an account. You can use any combination — for example, sign up with email and later add a passkey for faster logins.

## Email + password

The standard path. You'll need:

- A valid email address (we send a verification link).
- A password that's at least **12 characters**, with at least one uppercase letter, lowercase letter, digit, and special character.

If any of those rules aren't met, the signup form tells you exactly which one to fix.

After signup you'll receive a welcome email and a separate verification email. Verifying isn't required to use the app, but it unlocks password reset and login-notification features.

## Google sign-in

Click **Continue with Google** on the login page. Pick your Google account in the popup, and we'll create a FamNme account using that email.

- Your Google password never touches our servers.
- If you later set an email password too, you can use either method to sign in.

## Passkeys (recommended after initial signup)

Once you're signed in, go to **Settings → Account → Passkeys** and tap *Add passkey*. Your device creates a cryptographic key that's tied to your biometrics (Face ID, Touch ID, Windows Hello). The next time you sign in, you'll see a **Sign in with passkey** button — no password needed.

Passkeys are resistant to phishing and password-reuse attacks. They're the safest way to sign in to FamNme.

See [Managing passkeys](/help/account-privacy/managing-passkeys) for full details.

## Trouble?

- **"Password must contain…"** errors: count your characters again — the minimum is 12, not 8.
- **Signup returns an error**: if you see a generic "failed to create account" screen, refresh and try once more. The app shows the real reason (weak password, email already in use) as a short message near the form.
- **Didn't get the welcome email**: check spam. Welcome mail is non-critical — you can still use the app.
