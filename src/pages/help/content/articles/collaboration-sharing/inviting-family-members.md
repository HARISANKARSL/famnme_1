Collaborators are other FamNme users who can view or edit your tree. Inviting someone is link-based — you generate an invite, send it however you like, and they claim it.

## Sending an invite

1. Open **Collaborators** from the dashboard (Family section in the sidebar).
2. Click **+ Invite**.
3. Choose what you're inviting them to claim:
   - A **specific person** in the tree (they'll become that person once they accept).
   - Or a **role-only invite** (Viewer / Controller / Co-owner) without being tied to a node.
4. Pick a role: Viewer, Controller, or Co-owner. See [Understanding roles](/help/collaboration-sharing/understanding-roles).
5. Copy the link, or have FamNme email it to them.

The link is single-use and tied to the invitation record. If it's used or revoked, the link stops working.

## Tracking pending invites

The Collaborators panel shows three sections:

- **Pending** — link generated, not yet accepted. You can resend the email, copy the link, or revoke.
- **Active** — the person has accepted and can now access the tree.
- **Removed** — people whose access was revoked. Kept for audit; clear from this list at any time.

## Link hygiene

- Invites expire after **30 days** if not claimed. Regenerate if needed.
- Revoking an accepted collaborator removes their access immediately. Their contributions stay in the tree.
- The claim link grants the role you set — so if you share the link in a public place, anyone who sees it could claim. For private invites, prefer email.

## Invites you've received

If someone else invites you, you'll see a notification in the app and an email. Clicking the link opens the **Claim your node** flow. See [Claiming your node](/help/collaboration-sharing/claiming-your-node).
