When someone invites you to a specific person in their tree, accepting the invite **claims** that node as your identity inside that tree.

## The accept flow

1. You click the invite link — often from an email.
2. If you don't have a FamNme account, you're prompted to sign up first (the same email the invite was sent to).
3. After sign-in, you see a confirmation screen: *"You're being added as **Priya Sharma** in the Sharma family tree. Role: Controller."*
4. Click **Accept**. The node is now linked to your account.

Once accepted:

- Your avatar replaces the default initial on that node.
- You can edit your own record freely (name, photo, bio, etc.).
- Others' edits to *your* node are routed to a review queue — you approve or reject.
- The tree shows up in your Trees list.

## Viewer role + claim

Even viewers can claim a node. They just can't edit anything — the claim only gives them identity on that node.

## Can I decline?

Yes. On the confirmation screen, pick **Decline**. The invite is marked rejected; the tree owner is notified. Your account is not created in the process.

## Can I un-claim?

Yes — from Settings → Trees → the specific tree → **Release my node**. You stay as a collaborator (if you had a role) but the node reverts to unclaimed. You cannot un-claim if you're the sole co-owner of the tree.

## Multiple trees, multiple identities

You can be claimed on your own node in many trees at once. Each tree sees your identity through the node you claimed there. Your core profile (email, password) is global.
