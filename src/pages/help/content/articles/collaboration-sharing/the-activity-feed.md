The **Activity Feed** is a time-ordered log of every change in the current tree. Open it from Family → Activity in the sidebar.

## What gets logged

- Adding, editing, or deleting a person.
- Photo uploads and removals.
- Memory creation, edits, deletions.
- Collaborator invites, role changes, removals.
- Tree settings changes (home person, visibility, name).
- Merges and splits.

Reactions and replies on memories are **not** in the activity feed — they're in the memory's own thread.

## Filters

At the top of the feed you can filter by:

- **Who** — one or more collaborators.
- **What** — type of change (person edit, memory, etc.).
- **When** — preset ranges (today, 7 days, 30 days) or a custom range.
- **Which person** — filter to all changes affecting one specific relative.

## Reverting a change

Click the triple-dot on any activity row to see *Revert*. This is available only to co-owners and the tree owner. Reverts are themselves logged so the audit trail stays honest.

Some actions cannot be reverted from the feed — specifically hard-deletes (which scrub the underlying record) and tree-wide setting changes older than 30 days. Use the [Duplicate and backup workflow](/help/building-your-tree/deleting-or-duplicating-a-tree) if you need stronger safety.

## Subscribing to activity

Toggle **Notify me about activity in this tree** (Settings → Notifications → per-tree) to get a daily summary email of changes. Useful if you don't check the tree daily but want to stay informed.
