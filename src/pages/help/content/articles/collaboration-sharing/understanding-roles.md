Every collaborator on a tree has a single role. Roles are tree-specific: you can be a co-owner of your mother's tree and a viewer on your in-laws' tree simultaneously.

## The roles

### Viewer

- Read the tree — see people, relationships, memories.
- Cannot add, edit, or delete anything.
- Cannot invite others.
- Can react to memories and leave replies.

Use this for distant relatives or friends you want to show the tree to, without risk of accidental edits.

### Controller

Everything Viewer can do, plus:

- Add people, edit details, upload photos.
- Create, edit, and delete memories they authored.
- Suggest edits on people claimed by someone else (those edits go into a review queue for the claimed person).

Cannot delete people added by others, cannot invite new collaborators, cannot change tree settings.

### Co-owner

Everything Controller can do, plus:

- Delete any person, memory, or relationship.
- Invite new collaborators and manage roles (except the Owner).
- Edit tree settings (name, home person, visibility defaults).
- The tree appears as a default-eligible tree for them.

Cannot delete the tree itself or transfer ownership.

### Owner

The person who created the tree. All Co-owner permissions, plus:

- Delete the tree.
- Transfer ownership to another user (they become Co-owner; you become Co-owner).
- Hard-delete members (bypassing the ghost node flow).

## Claimed nodes and edits

If a collaborator has claimed a specific person in the tree, that person's details are edited primarily by them. Other contributors' changes go through a lightweight review queue on the claimed person. This balances openness with the claimed user's control over their own record. See [Claiming your node](/help/collaboration-sharing/claiming-your-node).

## Changing a role

The Owner or any Co-owner can change a collaborator's role from the Collaborators panel → triple-dot → Change role. The change takes effect immediately on their next action.
