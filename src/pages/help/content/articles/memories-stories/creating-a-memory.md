A **memory** in FamNme is a capsule of photos, a short story, and the people it involves. Memories are attached to people so they surface on each person's profile, and they appear chronologically in the Timeline and the Memory feed.

## Quick create

1. From the dashboard sidebar, click **Memories → + New memory**.
2. Give it a title (e.g., "Grandfather's 70th birthday").
3. Add a date (or a rough year, if unknown).
4. Drag in photos — up to 20 per memory.
5. Tag the people involved by typing their names; matches auto-suggest.
6. Write a short story in the body (optional but recommended).
7. Save.

## Slides

For a longer memory, switch from *Single* to *Slides* mode at the top. Each slide is a separate photo + caption combo. Viewers can swipe through.

- Slides are great for multi-day events (a wedding, a road trip).
- Captions can differ from the memory body; the body is the overall story, captions are per-photo.
- Reorder slides by dragging.

## Who sees it

By default, a memory inherits the visibility of the tree. You can override on a per-memory basis:

- **Family** — all tree collaborators.
- **Specific people** — pick individual collaborators who can view.
- **Only me** — a private journal entry.

## Editing later

Memories are editable. Open from the Memories page, click Edit. Photos can be added or removed; tags, date, title, and body can all change. Every edit is tracked in the memory's history.

## Deleting

The Delete button on the memory's Edit dialog removes it permanently. Unlike people, memories do not become ghost nodes — they're gone for good.
