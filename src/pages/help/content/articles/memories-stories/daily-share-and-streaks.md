Daily Share is a light, once-a-day prompt that surfaces an old memory or asks you to capture a new one. It's designed to make the tree feel alive without being pushy.

## What you see each day

A single card on the dashboard showing one of:

- **Throwback** — a memory from this date in a previous year.
- **Birthday / anniversary** — a relative has a date today.
- **Prompt** — a gentle question like *"What was grandmother's favorite recipe?"* to nudge new memories.
- **Heritage fact** — a cultural note tied to today (festival, historical date).

You can react (👍❤️🎉) or reply to the day's card.

## Streaks

Engaging with the daily card for consecutive days builds a **streak**. The streak count shows on your profile and in the Daily Share panel. Missing a day resets it.

Streaks are purely motivational — nothing unlocks or locks based on them.

## Notifications

You can choose to receive a daily email with the day's share, or a push notification if you've installed the PWA. Configure both under **Settings → Notifications**.

## Opting out

If daily prompts aren't for you, toggle **Show Daily Share card** off in Settings → Preferences. The card disappears from the dashboard; streak tracking stops.
