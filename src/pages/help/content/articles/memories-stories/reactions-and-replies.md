Every memory supports two lightweight ways to engage: reactions and replies.

## Reactions

Tap the emoji button on a memory card. Pick from ❤️, 😀, 😢, 🎉, 🙏, 👏. Reactions are visible to everyone who can see the memory; hover over the counter to see who reacted.

- One reaction per person per memory, but you can change yours anytime.
- Reactions don't generate email notifications (by default) — they're meant to be low-noise.

## Replies

The **Reply** button opens a text field. Keep replies short — a sentence or two. Longer stories should become their own memory.

- Replies nest one level deep (reply-to-reply).
- Owners can pin one reply per memory so it shows first.
- Edit / delete from the "…" menu on your own reply.

## Notifications

Whether you're emailed about reactions or replies depends on your settings:

- **Settings → Notifications → Reactions on my memories** — default: off.
- **Settings → Notifications → Replies to my memories** — default: on.
- **Settings → Notifications → Replies to threads I participate in** — default: on.

Turning all of these off keeps the app quiet; you'll only see engagement when you visit the memory.

## Moderation

Tree owners and co-owners can hide any reply that breaks house rules. The reply is kept in an audit log for 30 days, then permanently deleted.
