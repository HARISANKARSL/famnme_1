Adding a parent or child offers two paths:

## Link to an existing person

Use this when the relative is already in the tree elsewhere — say, their sibling's spouse or a distant cousin that's now becoming a direct ancestor. In the Add dialog, type their name and pick them from the suggestion list. FamNme creates the new relationship without duplicating the person.

Linking is reversible: you can always remove a relationship from the Edit panel later.

## Create a new person

Use this when the relative doesn't exist in the tree yet. Fill in the name and any known details; the new person is saved and the relationship connects them to the relative you started from.

## Parent rules

- **Biological parents**: up to two (typically one of each gender, but FamNme doesn't enforce this).
- **Adoptive / step parents**: use the *Relationship type* dropdown on the parent row. The canvas renders step-relationships with a dashed line.
- **Missing parents**: if you don't know both parents, you can leave one slot empty. The canvas won't draw a ghost in an empty slot.

## Children rules

- A child must be linked to at least one parent (that's the "family" relationship).
- A child can be linked to a **union** rather than a parent individually — useful when both parents are in the tree.
- You can reassign a child to a different union later from the child's Edit panel.

## Tips

- Before adding a new person, search for them first (Ctrl+K). Duplicate people are the most common data-quality issue in genealogy.
- If you realize you've created duplicates, the [Duplicate detection](/help/heritage-discovery/duplicate-detection-and-merging) tool can merge them.
