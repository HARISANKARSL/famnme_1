There are three ways to add a person:

1. **From the canvas**: right-click (or long-press) a relative and pick *Add parent / spouse / child / sibling*. The new person is linked to that relative automatically.
2. **From All People**: click the **+ Add person** button at the top right. You'll then attach them to an existing relative in the next step.
3. **From a Memory**: when creating a memory, typing a name that doesn't match anyone existing offers *Create "…" as a new person*.

## What's required

Only a **first name** is strictly required. Everything else is optional but makes the tree richer:

- Last name / family name.
- Gender.
- Birth date, birthplace.
- Death date (if applicable), deathplace.
- Occupation, education, religion.
- Biography / notes.

You can always come back and edit later.

## Profile photos

Photos upload to encrypted storage and are served via CDN. Supported formats: JPG, PNG, WEBP. Max 10 MB.

- **Desktop**: drag and drop into the photo area, or click to browse.
- **Mobile**: tap the photo area — you'll get a native sheet to choose Camera, Photos, or Files.
- **Crop**: after picking, a crop dialog lets you frame the face. You can skip this and adjust later.

No photo? FamNme renders a nice initial-based avatar using the person's first and last name.

## After adding

The person is drawn on the canvas inside the union or slot you picked. The canvas recalculates generation tiers automatically — occasionally, other people may shift slightly to accommodate the new node. That's expected.
