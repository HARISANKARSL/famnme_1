Any person's card has an **Edit** button (pencil icon). Opening it reveals the full editor with multiple tabs.

## Basic tab

- Full name, maiden name, gender.
- Birth / death dates and places.
- A primary photo.

## Cultural tab (optional but rich)

Tailored to Indian family contexts — though you can use or skip each field freely:

- **Religion** — free text; common values suggested.
- **Gotra** — Sanskrit lineage name (Kashyap, Bhardwaj, etc.); commonly recorded in Hindu families.
- **Ethnicity / community** — e.g., *Tamil Brahmin*, *Sindhi*, *Bengali*.
- **Nationality** — country of citizenship.
- **Mother tongue** — primary spoken language in the family.

These show up in search, statistics, and filters — for example you can quickly find all people of a specific gotra.

## Occupation & education

Free-text with autocomplete from your existing entries. Over time the dropdown learns your family's common jobs and schools.

## Biography

A longer narrative field. Supports paragraphs; no formatting. A good place to record how someone met their spouse, how they're remembered by the family, notable achievements.

## Change history

Every edit is tracked. From the person's profile click **History** (clock icon) to see who changed what and when. Owners and co-owners can revert a specific field to an earlier value.

See also: [Claiming your node](/help/collaboration-sharing/claiming-your-node), which determines who can edit *their own* details vs yours.
