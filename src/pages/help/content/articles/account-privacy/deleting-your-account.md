Deleting your account is a deliberate, two-step process. This prevents accidents and malicious deletions if someone gets brief access to your session.

## Step 1 — Request deletion

1. Go to **Settings → Account → Delete my account**.
2. Read the warning screen carefully.
3. Click **Request deletion**.
4. FamNme emails a confirmation link to your account email.

Nothing is deleted yet. You can change your mind at any time up to the final confirmation.

## Step 2 — Confirm via email

1. Open the email. The link expires in **1 hour**.
2. Click **Confirm deletion**.
3. Your account and all data owned solely by you is permanently deleted.

## What gets deleted

- Your user record (email, password hash, name, session history).
- Every tree you own — including all people, relationships, photos, memories, and stories inside.
- All collaborators' access to your trees.
- Your reactions and replies on others' memories.
- Passkeys registered to your account.

## What stays

- Memories and replies you authored on trees owned by others — they remain but are attributed to "Deleted user". This is because the other tree owner needs a coherent history; attribution to "Deleted user" preserves that while anonymizing you.
- Audit logs (security traces of logins and admin actions) are retained for 180 days to meet compliance retention, then purged.

## Trees co-owned with others

If you're a co-owner (not owner) of another tree, deleting your account just removes you as a collaborator — the tree itself is not affected.

If you're the sole owner of a tree and also have co-owners, deletion would orphan them. The confirmation screen will detect this and prompt you to either transfer ownership first or confirm you want the tree deleted along with your account.

## I changed my mind — too late?

Within an hour of the confirmation email, if you haven't clicked the link, your account is still fine — just ignore the email. The request expires automatically.

After clicking the final link, deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. We cannot restore a deleted account; this is by design.
