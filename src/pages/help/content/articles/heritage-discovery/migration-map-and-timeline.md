Two complementary views of your tree across space and time.

## Migration Map (`/migration`)

Plots each person's birthplace, residence, and deathplace on a world map, then draws arrows showing the moves across generations.

- Zoom into a region to see local density — which of your people were born in Kochi vs Pune.
- Toggle generations on/off to see movement over time (e.g., "show me where grandparents lived vs where we live now").
- Click any pin for the person's card.

Places are geocoded when you enter them. If a place doesn't resolve, the map shows a small warning on the person — you can correct the spelling or pick a candidate from the dropdown.

## Timeline

Chronological view of every life event: births, marriages, deaths, notable memories with dates. Open from Heritage → Timeline.

- Filter by person, generation, or event type.
- Scroll across decades; the timeline pans smoothly.
- Click any event to jump to the person or memory.

## Combining them

Both views respect the filters you've set. If you filter to your paternal line on Timeline, then open the Map, the same filter is applied — so you see paternal geography over paternal time.

## Privacy

Locations for living people respect each person's visibility. If a person is set to Private, their location isn't plotted even for collaborators (until you either change the visibility or they claim their node and enable location sharing).
