FamNme ships with a curated directory of over 2,600 Indian temples — Shakti peethas, Jyotirlingas, ancient Vaishnavite and Shaivite temples, Jain tirthas, Sikh Gurudwaras, and regionally-significant shrines.

## Browsing

Open **Temples** from the Heritage section of the sidebar. Filter by:

- **State** — from Kashmir to Tamil Nadu.
- **Primary deity** — Shiva, Vishnu, Devi, Ganesh, and many more.
- **Tradition** — Vaishnava, Shaiva, Shakta, Jain, Sikh, Buddhist.
- **Name** — freetext search.

Each temple card shows location, primary deity, a short cultural note, and (where available) a photo.

## Linking a temple to a person

On the temple's card, click **Link to family member** and pick a person. The link establishes that this temple is significant to them — ancestral shrine, pilgrimage completed, officiated there, or personal reason.

Once linked, the temple appears:

- On the person's profile under *Sacred places*.
- On the Migration Map as a small golden temple pin (optional toggle).
- As a filter option on Timeline and Memories.

## Temple-anchored memories

When you create a memory, you can pick a temple as the memory's location — useful for pilgrimage stories. Such memories are plotted on the Temple Map and aggregate into your family's pilgrimage trail.

## Missing a temple?

If a temple you care about isn't in our directory, submit it from Temples → *Suggest a temple* with name, location, deity, and a brief description. We add submissions to a monthly update batch.
