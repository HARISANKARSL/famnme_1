You can create as many trees as you want. They're fully isolated — people, memories, and collaborators in one tree never appear in another.

## Switching trees

The sidebar shows your current tree name at the top. Click it to open the tree switcher — every tree you own, or have been invited to, is listed there. Click another tree to switch; the canvas reloads.

## The Trees page (`/trees`)

A richer view of every tree you can access. For each tree you see:

- Tree name and cover photo.
- Member count, photo count, memory count.
- Your role (owner, co-owner, controller, viewer).
- Last activity.

From this page you can open any tree, mark one as your **default** (the one that loads on sign-in), rename, duplicate, or delete trees you own.

## Default tree

Your default tree is the one that loads when you open `/dashboard` without a specific tree context. By default it's the first tree you created. Change it by clicking the star icon on any tree card in `/trees`.

## Why multiple trees?

- **Separation of concerns** — keep your spouse's family separate from yours, then bridge them later if you want.
- **Sandbox experiments** — duplicate a tree before a big restructuring so you always have a safe copy.
- **Shared vs private** — a public tree for your wedding invitees, a private tree for closer records.

See also: [Deleting or duplicating a tree](/help/building-your-tree/deleting-or-duplicating-a-tree).
