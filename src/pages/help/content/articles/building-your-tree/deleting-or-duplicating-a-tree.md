## Duplicating a tree

From the Trees page, click the triple-dot menu on any tree you own and pick **Duplicate**. FamNme creates a full copy:

- Every person, relationship, and photo is copied.
- Memories and stories are copied.
- Collaborators are **not** copied — the duplicate starts with just you as the owner.
- The copy is named *"<original> (copy)"* and can be renamed immediately.

Duplicating is the safest way to experiment with a big restructuring. Work in the copy; if it goes well, delete the original and rename the copy back.

## Deleting a tree

Deletion is permanent. From Trees → triple-dot → **Delete tree** you'll see a confirmation dialog that requires you to type the tree name. This prevents accidental deletes.

What gets removed:

- All people, relationships, photos, memories, and stories inside that tree.
- All collaborator invitations and access records for that tree.
- The tree's cover photo and cached data.

What is **not** removed:

- Your account itself.
- Your other trees.
- Emails already sent to collaborators (they remain in their inboxes but the links will now 404).

There's no restore flow. If you're worried, duplicate first and keep the copy as a backup.

## Who can delete

Only the tree owner can delete a tree. Co-owners can edit everything else but cannot delete. If you need a co-owner to become the new owner, transfer ownership via **Settings → Trees → Transfer**.
