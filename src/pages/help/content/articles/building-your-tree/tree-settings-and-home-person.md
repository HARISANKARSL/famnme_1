Every tree has a settings panel. Open it from the gear icon next to the tree name in the sidebar, or from **Settings → Trees**.

## What you can change

- **Tree name** — a short, memorable label. Shown in the sidebar and the Trees page.
- **Home person** — the node generations are measured from. See below.
- **Default visibility** — whether newly added members are marked Private or Family-visible by default.
- **Cover photo** — shown on the Trees page card.

## Changing the home person

The home person is the "you are here" on the canvas. Changing it:

- Re-labels generations: the new home person becomes Generation 0 (Self). Their parents become -1, grandparents -2, and so on.
- Does **not** delete or reorder data. All relationships and records stay as they are.
- Updates the canvas layout so the new home person is centered.

If you delete the current home person, FamNme automatically reassigns the role to the next closest relative who remains. You'll see a banner confirming the reassignment.

## Multiple trees, multiple perspectives

Because the home person is per-tree, one person can be the home person of their own tree and a distant relative inside another family's tree at the same time. The perspective is whichever tree you're currently viewing.

## Privacy defaults

Under *Default visibility* you can pick what applies to newly added members:

- **Public** — visible to anyone with the tree link.
- **Family** — visible to invited collaborators only. Default.
- **Private** — visible only to the owner.

Existing members are unaffected by changing this setting — their visibility must be changed individually.
