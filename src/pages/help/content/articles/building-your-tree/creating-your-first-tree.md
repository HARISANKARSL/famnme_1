A tree in FamNme is a self-contained family unit. You can have several trees (one for your father's side, one for your mother's, one for your spouse's). Each tree has one **home person** — the root through which generations are measured.

## The new-tree wizard

1. On the dashboard, click **+ Create tree** (or go to *Trees* in the sidebar and click the same button).
2. Name the tree — e.g. *"Sharma family"* or *"Meera's maternal line"*.
3. Add the first person: this becomes the home person by default. Enter name, gender, birth date if known.
4. Optionally upload a photo.
5. Finish. The canvas opens with that person centered.

You can rename the tree or change the home person at any time from **Tree settings**.

## Choosing a good home person

The home person affects how generations are labeled ("parents", "grandparents", "children"). Pick the person whose perspective the tree most represents. Common choices:

- **Yourself** — most common. Useful when you're documenting your own ancestors and descendants.
- **An elder** — if the tree is mostly historical, making a grandparent or great-grandparent the home person centers the tree better visually.
- **A shared ancestor** — when two branches meet, set the home person to the common ancestor so both branches render outward.

You can change the home person later without losing data, but relationship labels in the UI will shift accordingly.
